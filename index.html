Zicheng Hu

M.S. student in Software Engineering at ECNU · Advisor: Prof. Cheng Chen

Research agenda. I develop practical online learning algorithms with provable guarantees, focusing on (i) robustness under non-stationarity / adversarial corruptions and (ii) decentralized / federated multi-agent bandits with efficient communication. I am also interested in bridging bandit theory to LLM alignment / RLHF (e.g., preference feedback as contextual/dueling bandits and reliable online/offline evaluation).
