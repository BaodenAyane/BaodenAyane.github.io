I am an M.S. student in Software Engineering at ECNU, Shanghai, China. My research focuses on online learning and multi-armed bandits, especially (i) robustness under non-stationarity/adversarial corruptions, and (ii) decentralized/federated bandits with communication constraints. I am also interested in bridging bandit theory to LLM alignment / RLHF (e.g., preference feedback as contextual/dueling bandits and reliable online/offline evaluation).

Research Interests

  • Robust bandits under adversarial corruptions and non-stationarity

  • Decentralized / federated multi-agent bandits (communication-efficient learning)

  • LLM alignment / RLHF from an online learning perspective (preference learning, OPE)

  • Recommendation and interactive learning (optional)

If you are interested in discussing or collaborating, feel free to contact me by email.
