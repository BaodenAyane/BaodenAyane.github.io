Research

My work centers on online learning / bandits with an emphasis on robustness, adaptivity, and decentralization. I am also actively exploring how these ideas can improve LLM alignment / RLHF and LLM-based recommendation systems.

Current Themes

  • Robust bandits under adversarial corruptions: corruption budgets, robustness trade-offs, high-probability guarantees.
  • Decentralized / federated bandits: communication-efficient coordination, topology dependence, scalability.
  • Bridging to RLHF: preference feedback as contextual/dueling bandits; sample-efficient, robust alignment updates; reliable online/offline evaluation.

Selected Projects

  • Corruption-robust bandit framework (NeurIPS 2025): scalable and parallelizable algorithms with regret guarantees under corruptions.
  • Byzantine-resilient decentralized bandits (AAAI 2026): robust learning over networks with adversarial agents/messages.
  • Best-of-both-worlds federated bandits (ICLR 2026): near-optimal performance across stochastic and adversarial regimes.

Prospective (2026) — RLHF/Alignment as Bandits

I am preparing a research project that formalizes RLHF pipelines as bandit problems and studies (i) robustness to noisy preference labels and distribution shift, and (ii) data-efficient online/offline evaluation.
