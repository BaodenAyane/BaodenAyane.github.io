Conference Publications

  • Theory of decision making porblems

    1. A Near-Optimal Best-of-Both-Worlds Algorithm for Federated Bandits
      Zicheng Hu, Zihao Wang, Cheng Chen, ICLR 2026.
      We propose a federated bandit algorithm with near-optimal regret guarantees in both stochastic and adversarial environments.

    2. Robust Decentralized Multi-armed Bandits: From Corruption-Resilience to Byzantine-Resilience
      Zicheng Hu, Yuchen Wang, Cheng Chen, AAAI 2026.
      We develop a decentralized bandit algorithm that is robust to adversarial corruptions and Byzantine agents, with theoretical guarantees and empirical validation.

    3. A Near-optimal, Scalable and Parallelizable Framework for Stochastic Bandits Robust to Adversarial Corruptions and Beyond
      Zicheng Hu, Cheng Chen, NeurIPS 2025.
      We propose a scalable and parallelizable framework for stochastic bandits robust to adversarial corruptions, achieving near-optimal regret up to logarithmic factors.

  • Auctions & Resource Allocation

    1. Collaborative resource allocation in computing power networks: A game-theoretic double auction perspective
      Yingzhuo Deng, Zicheng Hu, Weihao Xu, Ningning Han, Haibin Cai, Computer Networks.
      We formulate collaborative resource allocation in computing power networks as a double auction game and evaluate the proposed mechanism through extensive experiments.
